Now here's the thing nobody puts side by side.
Released is not the same as made. Everything on that page is what he decided to put out.
Behind it is the drive. The stuff nobody has ever heard, and nobody has ever been given a way to hear.
Go guess the number before I say it.

Hold that thought.

ANYWAY.

So how many beats has he made?
He estimated about half a million.

And here's the crazy part. Half a million pieces of music, and the business model available to him for almost all of it is nothing at all.
He cant release half a million beats. No feed on earth could carry it and no fan could listen to it.
But the people who love him would pay to get inside the drive. Not to own it. Just to be let in.

Now to be fair, most of that is loops and sketches and not finished songs, and half a million of anything is an estimate, not a receipt.
And a beat with no home is not automatically worth money. It's worth money to the specific people who already care.

But if what you already made is bigger than what you'll ever release, the question aint what do I put out next.
