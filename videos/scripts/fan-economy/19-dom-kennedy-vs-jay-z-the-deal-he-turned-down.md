# Dom Kennedy vs Jay-Z: the deal he turned down

**SCRIPT:**

Who had to wait longer to own his own records, Jay-Z or Dom Kennedy, and by how many years?
Let's find out.

Jay-Z is the reason half of us even talk about ownership.
In 1997 his label, Roc-A-Fella, went into business with Def Jam, and his masters ended up in the Def Jam system.
He built the leverage first, then used it to go get them back. He has called that the fight of his life, and it's the blueprint everybody quotes.

Dom Kennedy went the other way from the start.
His records come out on his own label, OPM.
In 2013 Interscope came for him, at the exact moment the industry was minting stars, and he passed.
People thought he was crazy. That was THE moment to sign.

Now here's what a deal actually is, and nobody says this part out loud.
A deal is a loan against a fanbase you have not built yet.
Somebody funds the rollout and takes a position in what comes back, and that position outlives almost every artist's hot streak.
That aint a scam, that's the trade. The only question is how long it takes to get back what you gave up.

So I put the two clocks side by side. Jay-Z's, and Dom Kennedy's.
Them two numbers dont even look like they came from the same business.

Real quick.
You already know how to market to fans. Now you need a market FOR fans.
The relationships, the sales, the list of who actually pays you, all in your name: that's what artists run on the CRWN app.
ANYWAY.

So who waited longer to own his own records, Jay-Z or Dom Kennedy, and by how much?
Jay-Z's masters came back to him in 2014. That's about 17 years after the Def Jam deal.
Dom Kennedy's number is zero. He never signed his records to a label, so there was nothing to get back.
Seventeen years of Jay-Z's fight got him what Dom Kennedy had on day one.
But them 17 years also built Jay-Z an empire and a reach Dom Kennedy has never had, and Dom Kennedy got to spend every one of his years building a fanbase instead of fighting for the tapes.

And that's not even the interesting half.
The deal Dom Kennedy turned down would have been OVER by now anyway.
Deals end. Executives leave. Priorities move. Thirteen years later that contract would be a memory, and the thing still standing is the exact thing he kept: the relationship with his fans.
In 2025 he ran an 18-city headline tour with about 487,000 people following him on Instagram.
He aint have to build an empire to get to ownership. He just started there.

Now to be fair, Jay-Z got every one of them masters back AND built a business independence cant buy, and most artists who sign never get either one. That fight was the harder thing to win.
And this is one clock, how long until you own your records. It says nothing about who made more.
