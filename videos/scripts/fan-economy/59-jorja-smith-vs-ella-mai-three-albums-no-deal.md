# Jorja Smith vs Ella Mai: three albums, no deal

**SCRIPT:**

Who has put out more albums since 2018, Ella Mai with a major label or Jorja Smith on her own?
Let's find out.

Nobody should pretend otherwise. It is a real career built a real way.

Jorja Smith came up at the same time, off the same wave of British R&B, and she went the other way.

So who has put out more albums since 2018, Ella Mai or Jorja Smith?
Neither one. Three each.
Ella Mai's three came with a major behind them, and her first one went double platinum in America.
Jorja Smith's three all came out on her own label, and two of them went top three in the UK.

And Jorja had a huge cosign early, which is not nothing and not repeatable on demand.
