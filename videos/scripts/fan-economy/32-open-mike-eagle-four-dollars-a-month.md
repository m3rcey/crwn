# Open Mike Eagle: four dollars a month

How many monthly listeners would he need to make that same money?

And 1,071 people pay him that every month, whether he puts out a record or not.

Now here's the thing nobody puts side by side.
Streaming pays somewhere around a penny a month per monthly listener. That's the discovery engine doing its job and it does it well.
So a thousand people at four dollars is worth how many of them?

Go do that division before I say it.

Hold that thought.
You dont need to market to fans. You need a market FOR fans.
Most artists are trying to grow the top number when the bottom one is the one that pays rent.
That's what the CRWN app is built around: membership tiers and a vault your top members pay to get into.
ANYWAY.

So how many monthly listeners does 1,071 people at four dollars equal?
About 428,000.

And here's the crazy part. That's 400 times fewer people for the same money.
One thousand people he can name, email and thank, against four hundred and twenty eight thousand he'd never meet.
And his thousand pay again next month without him doing anything. The 428,000 have to come back on they own.

Now to be fair, four dollars is a floor, not a ceiling. That's just where his membership starts, so the real number is higher than the math I just did.
And streaming is how most of them found him in the first place. Nobody's saying turn it off.

But if a thousand people at coffee money beats four hundred thousand listeners, the question aint how do I get more listeners.
It's how many of the ones I already got would pay four dollars, and why have I never asked.
