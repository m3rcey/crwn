# Larry June vs The Weeknd: an address beats a listener

**SCRIPT:**

Whose fan is worth more in a year, one of The Weeknd's listeners or one of Larry June's customers?
Let's find out.

The Weeknd's numbers are almost hard to talk about. Hundreds of millions of people, records that live on every platform at once, one of the biggest streaming artists in history.
That reach is real and it's the hardest thing in music to build. Nobody is arguing that.

Now Larry June.
Independent, his own lane, and he runs Midnight Organic, the whole healthy-living brand. Merch, product, the orange juice thing.
His people dont just stream Larry June. They check out. They put in a card, a name, and a shipping address.

And that right there is the difference nobody prices.
A streaming listener is a number. A customer is a NAME and an ADDRESS.
But there's a level under that, and it's the one that actually pays. It aint that somebody bought once. It's whether the same person grabbed the juice, then a shirt, then came back six months later for a record, and whether anything anywhere knows them four orders belong to one human being.
For most artists selling right now the answer is no. Four orders, three platforms, one fan nobody can see.

Now be clear about what that aint. That aint a knock on streaming, and it aint about who's bigger.
Streaming is doing its job, which is putting an artist in front of people who never heard them. That job is enormous and it's the reason any of this starts.
But a listener is somebody who might come back. A customer already proved they will pay.

So I priced the two units against each other. One customer, one listener, same year.
The gap is embarrassing.

Quick sidenote.
You already know how to market to fans. Now you need a market FOR fans.
The move aint choosing between them, it's making sure the reach turns into somebody you can actually reach back.
Memberships, direct sales, and every buyer and contact in one place in your name: that's what artists run on the CRWN app.
ANYWAY.

So whose fan is worth more in a year, The Weeknd's listener or Larry June's customer?

Streaming pays out around a penny a month per monthly listener. So one listener is about 12 cents a year.
One person buying a $60 piece of merch, one time, in a year, is $60.
That's one customer worth about 500 listeners. Not 500 streams. Five hundred PEOPLE, for a year.

But the money is the small half of it.
Nobody can email a streaming listener. Not Larry June, not The Weeknd, not anybody, because the platform was never built to hand a relationship over.
A customer with an address is the one channel that cannot be switched off, rate limited, or reshuffled by an algorithm update.
The Weeknd's reach is a miracle. It's just rented, at every size. An address is owned.

Them are illustrative numbers on a public streaming rate, not anybody's books.
So if you sell anything at all, even a couple shirts a month:
you already got names and addresses, sitting in two or three different places. When was the last time you put them side by side and looked at who shows up more than once?

I built a free calculator that shows what the audience you already own could be worth.
Comment OWN and I'll DM you the link.
