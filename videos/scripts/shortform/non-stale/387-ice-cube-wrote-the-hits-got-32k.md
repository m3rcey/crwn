**TOPIC:** Ice Cube wrote much of N.W.A's catalog (writing for Dr. Dre and Eazy-E). The group sold ~3 million records and toured (grossing ~$650K), yet Cube was reportedly paid about $32,000 and suspected manager Jerry Heller was withholding royalties he couldn't audit. He left in 1989 and released "AmeriKKKa's Most Wanted" (1990). Surprise: the writer of the hits got $32K off millions. Mechanism: he couldn't see the books, so the lack of transparency drove him out.
