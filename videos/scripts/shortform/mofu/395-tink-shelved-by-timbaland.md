**TOPIC:** Timbaland signed Tink at 18 (2014) to Mosley Music Group via an Epic deal, then shelved her finished debut album "Think Tink." She stayed stuck for years, owning nothing she could release. End of 2017 she exited the deal keeping rights to her unreleased music, started her own imprint Winter's Diary (2019, via Empire), and released independently. Winter's Diary 5 (July 12, 2024) hit #140 on the Billboard 200 as a self-owned release. Surprise/loss reversal: the label sat on her music for years; once she owned it she charted a whole album herself. CRWN capability: own your release + drop when you want, straight to fans.
