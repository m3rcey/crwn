And no hit single. No radio smash.

Every plan you get handed says you need a hit, and a hit means reach, the whole world hearing you one time. Streaming does that job well. But look at what Skyzoo has instead: the same people, buying the next one, for twenty years. Reach asks how many people heard it once. Loyalty asks how long somebody stays.

So I priced one of those people. When I lined it up next to a streaming number it stopped being close.

So how much is one fan who never leaves actually worth?

One fan on a $10 a month membership, for twenty years, is $2,400. To make that same $2,400 off streaming at about $0.003 a play, you'd need around 800,000 plays.

And here's the crazy part. You can't go find 800,000 streams on purpose. That's a lottery ticket. You can go find one person who would stay. That's a job you can do on a Tuesday.

To be fair, that math assumes a fan who stays the whole time, and real life is messier. But if you've been selling for years without the hit, somewhere in your orders is a person who has bought from you six times, and nothing you sell lets them give you more than a first-timer does.
