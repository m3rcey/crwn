# Fan Economy case-study artist pool

Owned by `/crwn-fan-economy`. **NON-AUTHORITATIVE on every number.** Entries are candidates whose
fit was assessed against [docs/ICP.md](../../docs/ICP.md) Tier 1 (250k-5M followers, 100k-3M
monthly listeners, deep catalog, PROVEN direct-to-fan behavior). Audience counts move monthly and
are deliberately NOT stored here: web-verify the metric you need at generation time. "Known
behavior" lines are research leads, not facts to assert; confirm before putting one in a script.

Rotation is derived from `videos/scripts/fan-economy/` filenames, not tracked here.

Rules recap: no megastars (the hard ICP pivot must not be absurd) · match artist to concept ·
`upper-bound` entries get an explicit pivot sanity check · hip hop and R&B first · if you add an
artist, add the full entry in the same session.
