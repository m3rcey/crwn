Pitched 2026-08-21. Fifty new case studies. No artist reused as subject or foil from scripts 1-40,
and none reused from batch 01 or batch 02, built or passed: 88 distinct names were excluded before
a single pitch was written.

checkable number. Batch 01 lost both picks at research and batch 02 lost three candidates, so the
