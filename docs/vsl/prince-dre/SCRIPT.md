# Prince Dre VSL: script (about 1:45)

In Dre's voice (Josh's edit, 2026-10-03). It plays on the drop page right after a fan claims a
free song, and in the video slot on his Gold and Platinum offers. One job: turn that fan into a
paying member.

## Evergreen: film it once (Josh, 2026-10-03)

**This video must stay true whatever projects Dre has out**, so he never re-films it when the
catalog changes. Projects move between levels (a pending plan already moves Stompin Thru The
Trenches into Gold and reorders the monthly projects), new ones get added, counts grow. So:

- **The video never names a project, a song count, or which tape sits on which level.** It names
  what each level DOES. The tier cards directly under the player say what is in each one today,
  and they update themselves.
- **The open loop is "music aint nobody ever heard"**, not one tape. Whatever is unreleased at the
  time is the answer, and the close points at the level that always has all of it.
- **Prices are the one thing it says that could change.** They are founder-set and rarely move,
  and saying "ten dollars" out loud sells. If a price ever changes, re-film only the HOW IT WORKS
  section; it is cut as its own take for that reason.

**What keeps it true** (if one of these stops being true, the video lies):

1. Platinum always holds every project, the day a fan joins, and hears new music first.
2. The unreleased music, videos and photos live on CRWN and nowhere else. Never post vault
   material to YouTube or Instagram before members have it.
3. Silver always has full projects in it, plus behind the scenes, the vote and the number.
4. Gold always gets more on day one than Silver, the Vault, and more projects over time.
5. The Vault has something in it before the video goes live.

## Every claim, against the offer (checked 2026-10-03)

| Line | Where it is true | Stays true as long as |
|---|---|---|

| A lot of projects, in order, one place | All his projects on his page, in release order | Always |
| $10: full projects, front to back | Silver holds complete projects (today Blood Brothaz, Life I Live, Streets Dont Love You) | Rule 3 |
| Behind the scenes, pictures | Silver: "Behind the scenes from every era" | Rule 3 |
| Vote on the unreleased stuff | Silver: "Vote on which unreleased songs Dre puts out next" | Rule 3 |

| $25: more projects day one, the vault, more the longer you stay | Gold: two projects on day one, the Vault, one more project after each of the first three months | Rule 4 |
| $50: everything without waiting, the hard-to-find ones, new music first | Platinum: all projects today, O Block Ass Nigga and Stompin, first listen | Rule 1 |

| Cancel whenever | Every paid offer: "Cancel any time. You keep the month you paid for." | Always |

"More projects open up the longer you stay" was chosen over "another project every month you
stay": Gold's monthly projects run out after month three today, so "every month" would stop
being true for a member in month four.

## Left out on purpose

- **The $19.99 tape, the $3.99 songs and the $250 / $150 credits.** One video, one ask: a
  membership. Each of those sells on its own page.
- **Annual pricing** ($8, $19, $37 a month paid yearly). True, but it is a second decision inside
  the first one. The tier cards and the FAQ already carry it.
- **Any project name or count.** See "Evergreen" above.

## Before Dre films

1. **The vault lines have to be true when the video goes up.** "Pictures nobody ever seen" and
   "videos that never came out" are promises. Dre should have at least a few of each ready to
   post the week the video goes live, so the first fan who joins finds something there.
2. **He can loosen any wording into how he really talks**, as long as he does not add a project
   name, a song count or a date. The prices must stay exact.
3. Look into the lens, not at the script. One take per section is fine; the cuts go at the labels.
