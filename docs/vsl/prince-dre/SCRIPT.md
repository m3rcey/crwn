# Prince Dre VSL: script (about 1:55)

Rewritten 2026-10-03 from his live offer
([princeDre.ts](../../../src/lib/offerExperience/reference/princeDre.ts), production read back the
same day: Bronze free, Silver $10, Gold $25, Platinum $50, 10 projects, Stompin products live) and
what Dre plans to put in: behind the scenes from his eras, unreleased videos, unreleased songs and
photos nobody has seen.

It plays on the drop page right after a fan claims a free song, and in the video slot on his Gold
and Platinum offers. One job: turn that fan into a paying member.

**What changed from the 2026-10-02 version, and why**

- **The open loop is Stompin Thru The Trenches, not "something on my phone from [YEAR]".** The old
  hook was written before Dre had anything that was actually scarce, so it leaned on one item Dre
  still had to pick, plus a "first drop this month" payoff the offer never promised. Stompin is
  real, live, 16 songs, and the one tape that exists nowhere else. The loop now closes on
  something a fan can press play on the moment they join.
- **"Every month I'm putting something in here" is gone.** The offer promises no schedule for the
  vault material ("as Dre adds them"), and CRWN never promises a cadence the artist did not choose.
  The only "every month" left is Gold's project drip, which the product enforces.
- **The catalog is 10 projects and 114 songs**, not 8 and 94.
- **The material from every era is now the reason to stay**, placed on the rung that delivers it:
  behind the scenes and photos at $10, a vote on the unreleased songs at $10, unreleased videos
  and cuts (the Vault) at $25.
- **The supporter number is in.** It is the one honest urgency the offer has: the earlier you
  join, the lower your number, and it never changes.

## Left out on purpose

- **The $19.99 tape, the $3.99 songs and the $250 / $150 credits.** One video, one ask: a
  membership. Each of those sells on its own page (the album page and the credits page), and a
  fourth price in a two-minute video makes the three that matter harder to hold.
- **Annual pricing** ($8, $19, $37 a month paid yearly). True, but it is a second decision inside
  the first one. The tier cards and the FAQ already carry it.
- **Listening sessions and first listen at Platinum.** Real, but Stompin is the reason a fan picks
  $50, and a second reason in the last ten seconds would dilute it.

## Before Dre films

1. **The vault lines have to be true when the video goes up.** "Pictures nobody ever seen" and
   "videos that never came out" are promises. Dre should have at least a few of each ready to
   post the week the video goes live, so the first fan who joins finds something there.
2. **He can loosen any wording into how he really talks.** These have to stay exact: the prices,
   "sixteen songs", "ten projects", the hundred and fourteen, and that Stompin is only at fifty.
3. Look into the lens, not at the script. One take per section is fine; the cuts go at the labels.
